# Icon and wordmark options

Open this folder in Finder to see the previews. Each option has an `.svg` (the source) and a `.png` (a 512 px render). Tell Claude which one you want and it will replace the launcher icon, splash, and in-app wordmark.

## Icons

| File | Idea | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `icon-1-peak-sun` | Two green ridges, ocean below, the ʻilima dot as a low sun on the summit line | Warm and friendly. Reads well small. |
| `icon-2-ridge-dot` | One serrated sea-glass ridge on night blue, the dot floating over the summit | The most minimal. Very memorable, almost a logo. |
| `icon-4-lens` | A round sight (kilo means to observe) with a mountain inside and the dot as the fix | Says "look closely". Busier at 48 px. |
| `icon-6-pin-peak` | The mountain is the pin: a diamond that narrows to a point, dot at the summit | Clever, a little abstract. |
| `icon-7-horizon` | What you see in the sky view: ridges, a horizon line, the dot where you are | Literal, calm. |
| `icon-8-wave-peak` | A mountain and a wave sharing one curve, the dot where they meet | Hawaiʻi in two shapes. Distinct from other map apps. |
| `icon-9-ridge-dusk` | Like 2 but with a dusk gradient and a soft glow on the dot | Richer version of the minimal one. |
| `icon-10-summit-mark` | A single mountain and a dot on sea blue | The simplest possible mark. Works as a favicon too. |

Dropped: a crater from above (read as an eye) and a flat island blob (read as a bean).

## Wordmarks

All are built from the real Fraunces (serif) and Inter (sans) outlines the app already bundles, so they match the UI. Each comes in `-dark` (sea glass on night) and most in `-light`.

| File | What the o does |
|---|---|
| `wordmark-fraunces-dot` | The o keeps its shape; the ʻilima dot sits in the counter. Subtle. |
| `wordmark-fraunces-pin` | The o grows a small point below the baseline and becomes a map marker. No color. |
| `wordmark-fraunces-pin-dot` | The marker o with the ʻilima dot inside. The most "map". |
| `wordmark-fraunces-ring` | The o is replaced by a clean ring and dot, a pin seen from above. |
| `wordmark-fraunces-ilima-o` | The whole o in ʻilima. Loud and simple. |
| `wordmark-inter-*` | Same ideas in Inter for a more modern, app-store feel. |

Pairings that work: icon 2 or 9 with `fraunces-dot`; icon 10 with `inter-ring`; icon 8 with `fraunces-pin-dot`.
